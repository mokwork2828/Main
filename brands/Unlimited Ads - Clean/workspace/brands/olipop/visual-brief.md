# OLIPOP — Visual Brief

## The Brand
A prebiotic soda for people who want real soda taste without the guilt — "A New Kind of Soda" that delivers nostalgia, gut health, and unapologetic fun in equal measure.
Voice: playful, irreverent, nostalgic, approachable, guilt-free

## Color System
Lead:        Sherwood Green — #034638
Support:     Warm Cream — #FAF7F0
Accent:      Flavor-coded pastels (pink, purple, orange, blue — varies by SKU)
Background:  Off-White / Pale Cream — #F5EFE0
Off-limits:  Clinical white, cold steel blue, neon brights, corporate greys

## How They Shoot
Light:    Soft, warm-diffused studio or natural daylight — bright and even, no hard shadows
Grade:    Warm-neutral, gently matte, moderate contrast, slightly lifted highlights — nostalgic not heavy
Frame:    Can upright and centered as hero; tabletop or lifestyle setups with generous negative space
Setting:  Vintage surfaces — aged stone, worn wood, pastel-painted plaster, retro diner counter
In frame: Glassware with poured soda and ice, citrus slices, retro straws, occasional soda shop props
Feel:     Joyful, nostalgic, refreshing

## The Product
Form:       12oz aluminum can, standard cylindrical
Label:      Full wraparound design; distinct color and minimalist illustration per flavor; brand name prominent across top
Signature:  Flavor-coded color system — each can is instantly identifiable by its unique hue, with a small retro illustration anchoring the flavor story

## Voice in Practice
Sounds like: "Real soda taste. Real good for you. What took so long?"
Never:       "Formulated with clinically-proven prebiotic compounds to support gut microbiome diversity."

## Hard Rules
Always: The can is the hero — label facing forward, clearly legible
Always: Warmth and joy in every frame — this brand makes people smile
Never:  Sterile, clinical, or pharmaceutical aesthetic
Never:  Cold, desaturated, or high-fashion minimalism — OLIPOP is playful, not austere

## Generation Modifier
Shoot with a 50–85mm lens, bright warm-diffused studio light — natural daylight quality, soft and even with no hard cast shadows. Color grade: warm-neutral, gently matte lift, moderate contrast, nostalgic but never heavy or vintage-filtered. Background: a vintage-toned surface in a coordinating muted tone — aged stone, worn wood, or pastel-painted plaster. Can upright and centered with joyful retro props: glassware with ice and fizz, citrus slices, retro straws. Mood: playful, nostalgic, joyful, refreshing.
