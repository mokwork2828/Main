## Aesthetic Reference — Polène Paris

### What This Brand Actually Does
Polène shoots entirely outdoors in vast, atmospheric landscapes. This is not an indoor studio brand. Their visual world is expansive, cinematic, and environmental. The bag exists as a natural object in a real world — not a product on a surface.

---

## Color-to-Background System

| Bag Color | Environment |
|---|---|
| Warm cognac / rust / tan | Dry earth, desert sand, arid hills — warm ochre foreground, hazy cool background |
| Neutral beige / cream | Beach, water's edge, pale washed-out sky — bright and open |
| Black / dark charcoal | Coastal cliffs, misty mountains, grey-blue water — cool and moody |
| Navy / deep tones | Cool atmospheric landscapes, overcast light, muted cool earth |

**The rule**: Light bags live in light open spaces. Dark bags live in cool moody environments. Warm bags anchor warm earth tones. Background always contrasts or complements the bag's value — the bag always reads clearly.

---

## Lighting System
- **Source**: Natural outdoor light only — no studio, no artificial
- **Type**: Golden-hour side/backlight OR flat overcast diffusion. Never flat midday sun
- **Temperature**: Warm golden light for earth-tone bags; cool blue-hour or overcast for dark bags
- **Quality**: Atmospheric and environmental — shadows are long, soft, never harsh
- **Depth**: Strong atmospheric perspective — sharp foreground, textured midground, hazy desaturated background

---

## Model Direction
- **Posture**: Upright, confident, grounded. Never posed or fashion-forward
- **Expression**: Serene, contemplative, looking into the distance or off-camera. Never smiling or performative
- **Clothing**: Monochromatic and tonal — beige, white, tan, black, cream. Oversized silhouettes, linen, knitwear, loose layers. Minimal accessories
- **Cropping**: Waist-up or full-body (feet visible). Never extreme face close-ups
- **Positioning**: Off-center, lower-middle or lower-third of frame. Sky dominates above

---

## Product-Only Shots
- Placed on natural organic surfaces: weathered rock, dried grass, driftwood, rope, earth
- Three-quarter angle showing bag form and strap detail
- Bag fills 40–60% of frame — significant environment remains visible
- Never on sterile or staged surfaces

---

## Composition Rules
- Sky occupies 50–70% of frame
- Horizon sits in lower third or centered
- Figure or bag positioned off-center — lower-right or lower-left quadrant
- Strong depth: foreground sharp, background atmospheric and hazy
- Aspect ratio mix: vertical (portrait, emphasises sky and scale) and horizontal (landscape depth)
- No props beyond the natural environment — kites, sails, ropes only when contextually real

---

## What They Never Do
- No studio backgrounds, plain walls, or interior settings
- No extreme close-ups of product detail in isolation
- No smiling, laughing, or performative energy
- No saturated, artificial, or heavily processed color
- No busy or cluttered compositions
- No winter/snow or tropical environments — temperate, moorland, arid, or coastal only
- No groups interacting. If multiple models, they are separate and composed
- No bag being opened, zipped, or used functionally
- No bright artificial lighting of any kind
