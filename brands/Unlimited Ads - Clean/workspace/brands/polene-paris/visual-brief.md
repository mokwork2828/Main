# Polène Paris — Visual Brief

## Brand Overview
Polène is a Parisian leather goods brand founded in 2016 by siblings Elsa, Antoine, and Mathieu Mothay. Every bag is handcrafted in small batches in Ubrique, Spain using Spanish and Italian calfskin. Priced between $300–$450, the brand occupies a precise gap: genuine luxury quality without the conglomerate markup or logo signaling. Their customer is discerning, values craftsmanship over branding, and has likely grown disillusioned with traditional luxury houses.

## Color Palette
- Primary: Warm Beige — #E8DECE
- Secondary: Cognac — #9B5E3A
- Accent: Bordeaux — #6B2737
- Neutral Dark: Sienna — #A0522D
- Text: Near Black — #1A1A18
- Background: Ivory / Cool Bone — #F5F0E8

## Typography
- Headlines: All-caps custom serif or refined sans-serif — sharp, confident, graceful
- Body: Elegant light-weight sans-serif, generous tracking
- Style notes: Minimal text on visuals. When copy appears, it is sparse, unhurried, and lowercase-leaning in feel even when set in caps

## Photography Style
Soft, diffused natural light with no hard shadows — images feel like they were taken near a large north-facing window or outdoors on overcast days. Backgrounds are architectural neutrals: raw plaster, warm concrete, matte stone, aged linen. Compositions are spare with generous negative space; the bag is never competing for attention. Skin tones (when models appear) are warm and unretouched-feeling. The overall mood is calm, unhurried, and quietly expensive.

## Visual Rules
**Always:**
- Generous negative space — let the product breathe
- Neutral, matte backgrounds (stone, plaster, linen, bone)
- Soft, natural or studio-diffused lighting — no flash, no harsh shadows
- One hero product per frame
- Texture is everything — show the grain of the leather

**Never:**
- Logos, wordmarks, or visible branding on the product itself
- Bright, saturated, or high-contrast color palettes
- Busy or cluttered compositions
- Fashion-forward styling that dates the image
- Flashy hardware or metallic accents

## Brand Voice
Understated and precise — Polène says exactly as much as needed, no more. Copy reads like a whisper: declarative, unhurried, free of superlatives. The brand never shouts.

## Target Customer
Primarily women, 25–40, based in major cities. They could afford Celine or Loewe but they are skeptical of logo culture and conglomerate fashion. They research before they buy, value craftsmanship and provenance, and discover brands through word of mouth and editorial. They describe Polène bags to friends in terms of how they feel, not how much they cost.

## Generation Modifier
Soft diffused natural light, warm neutral tones, matte stone or plaster background, single hero product, generous negative space, high-resolution leather texture detail, no text, editorial quiet luxury, Parisian minimalism, unhurried composition
