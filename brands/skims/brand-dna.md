BRAND DNA DOCUMENT
==================

## BRAND OVERVIEW

**Name:** SKIMS
**Tagline:** "Solutionwear for every body"
**Full Positioning Statement:** "Solutions for Every Body" — inclusivity as a product principle from day one, not a marketing afterthought.
**Design Agency:** King & Partners (comprehensive brand refresh, visual system, site redesign)
**Founded:** June 2019 (launched September 2019), Los Angeles, CA
**Co-Founders:** Kim Kardashian, Emma Grede, Jens Grede
**Valuation:** $5+ billion (2025–2026)
**Logo Origin:** Hand-sketched by Kanye West; Kim credited him as "ghost creative director" for early branding, model selection, and packaging direction.

**Voice Adjectives [5]:**
1. Understated — confidence expressed through restraint, not volume
2. Inclusive — every body type, skin tone, and size is the default, not an addition
3. Confident — never apologetic; the product solves real problems
4. Modern — culturally fluent, not trend-chasing
5. Luxurious — premium without being cold or inaccessible

**Positioning:** SKIMS redefined the shapewear and loungewear category by making radical inclusivity the starting point of product design. The brand operates at the intersection of celebrity culture and premium minimalism — orchestrating cultural moments rather than running traditional advertising. It beat Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and Prada in Launchmetrics' 2023 Media Impact Value rankings.

**Competitive Differentiation:**
- vs. Savage X Fenty: SKIMS is minimalist and understated (designed to disappear under clothes); Fenty is maximalist and expressive (designed to be seen)
- vs. Spanx: SKIMS is soft, loungewear-like, lifestyle-forward; Spanx is firm compression and performance-targeted
- vs. Victoria's Secret: SKIMS centers inclusion and real bodies; VS historically pushed a singular beauty ideal
- Unique advantage: Custom proprietary bubble wordmark, 9+ skin tone shade ranges, XXS–5XL sizing, NikeSKIMS collaboration, $5B+ cultural brand status

## VISUAL SYSTEM

**Primary Font (Logo Wordmark):** Custom proprietary bubble typeface — hand-originated by Kanye West. Bold, uppercase, soft rounded contours with a slight slant. The two "S" letters intentionally don't match, giving it a humanistic, hand-made quality. Soft and feminine with irregular details that feel custom vs. machine-made. Closest commercial equivalents: TPG DontBlurry, Spilled Ink, Outahere (with heavy modification).

**Secondary Font (Body / UI):** T-Star Pro — a proprietary technical sans-serif available in 10 weights (Light through Heavy Italic). Slender proportions, low-contrast uniform line width, laterally flattened round forms, cut-back ascenders and descenders for space efficiency. Clean, modern, readable. Used for all website UI, product descriptions, and body copy.

**Primary Color:** Taupe — #62554a. The dominant logo and brand color. Neutral, warm, inclusive — deliberately chosen to avoid favoring any skin tone.

**Secondary Colors (warm neutral palette):**
- #e1c4a7 (warm taupe)
- #e4c9af (soft taupe)
- #e9d3bf (light warm taupe)
- #efdfd0 (pale taupe / near-cream)
- #ebd7cf (muted pink-taupe)

**Background Colors:** Clean white (#FFFFFF) and soft off-white/cream neutrals. Generous whitespace. No dark backgrounds in primary brand expression.

**Accent / CTA Color:** Dark taupe to near-black neutrals consistent with #62554a brand tone. CTAs are high-contrast against light backgrounds — likely near-black or deep taupe text on white, or white text on dark taupe. Never bright or aggressive colors.

**Logo Versions:** Two — a flat design in official taupe brand colors, and a glossy gradient version resembling nail polish (used for premium/special contexts).

## PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

**Lighting:** Professional studio lighting with warm, flattering tones. High-production value with soft, even illumination that complements all skin tones. Not harsh or high-contrast — warm, enveloping, luxurious light. Feels premium without being cold.

**Color Grading:** Natural yet polished. Warm-toned, skin-flattering grade. Muted, not oversaturated. Colors stay close to real life — the aesthetic reward comes from composition and talent, not heavy post-processing.

**Composition:** Clean and spacious. Models centered or asymmetrically placed with generous negative space. Product detail shots show texture and material quality close-up. Full-body and three-quarter crops used for inclusive sizing storytelling. Editorial framing without feeling untouchable.

**Subject Matter:** Diverse models across age, ethnicity, body shape, and size. Celebrity talent (Hailey Bieber, Cardi B, Sabrina Carpenter, Tate McRae, Addison Rae, Jennifer Lopez, Lana Del Rey) alongside non-celebrity diverse talent. The product is always the hero — worn naturally, not styled aggressively.

**Props and Surfaces:** Minimal. Clean studio backgrounds in white, cream, or soft neutral tones. Occasional lifestyle/environmental sets for campaign work (vintage pin-up sets, winter-themed sets, athletic environments for NikeSKIMS). Day-to-day product photography is studio-only, clean surface, no distractions.

**Mood:** Comfortable confidence. Quiet luxury. Warmth without softness. The visual feeling of something premium that also actually solves a problem. Aspirational without being alienating.

**Notable Campaign Photographers:** Nick Knight, Nadia Lee Cohen, Luis Alberto Rodriguez, Rob Woodcox, Janicza Bravo (director).

## PRODUCT DETAILS (SKIMS Category Reference)

**Core Categories:** Shapewear, Underwear, Loungewear, Swimwear, Adaptive collection, NikeSKIMS performance line

**Material Philosophy:** Proprietary, skin-tone-matched fabrications. Soft, stretch, form-fitting. Premium hand-feel. SKIMS fabrics are designed to feel like a second skin — the packaging and visual system reflect this through softness in every touchpoint.

**Packaging System:**
- Matte-finish boxes with embossed SKIMS logo
- Custom-fit foam inserts with neatly folded garments
- Soft-touch bags and magnetic closure boxes for premium tiers
- Premium tissue paper in signature neutral brand colors
- Custom logo seals and stickers
- Thank-you cards included
- Satin ribbons for gift-ready presentation
- Seasonal thematic variations
- 85% of customers report sharing unboxing on social media — packaging is designed as a content moment

**Distinctive Visual Features:** The rounded, humanistic bubble wordmark on all touchpoints. Neutral taupe colorway on packaging. Matte finishes throughout. Nothing loud or brash — every detail signals premium restraint.

## AD CREATIVE STYLE

**Typical Formats:** Full-bleed model photography with minimal text. Clean white background product shots. Campaign-style editorial imagery with celebrity talent. Short-form video with authentic UGC energy. "Drop" style urgency ads for limited collections. Testimonial and review-driven social ads.

**Text Overlay Style:** Minimal. When text appears: T-Star Pro or similar clean sans-serif, light-to-regular weight, high letter-spacing on uppercase labels. Copy is short and declarative — rarely more than 5–7 words. Taupe or black on white; white on darker backgrounds. No decorative type treatments.

**Photo vs. Illustration:** Photography only. No illustration. High-production studio photography and campaign imagery dominate. UGC content is whitelisted and amplified for paid social.

**UGC Usage:** Central to strategy. SKIMS actively recruits, whitelist-amplifies, and partners with micro and macro influencers. The brand blurs the line between organic and paid — UGC content runs as paid ads and feels native to the feed.

**Offer Presentation:** Drop-style urgency ("Selling fast," "Limited edition") rather than discount-driven promotion. Rarely leads with price cuts. Scarcity and cultural relevance are the conversion levers, not percentage-off banners.

**3 Core Ad Storylines:**
1. Comfort as Confidence — how SKIMS makes you feel secure and capable
2. Representation as Design — diverse bodies are the design brief, not the tagline
3. Simplicity as Luxury — minimalism and restraint as premium signals
