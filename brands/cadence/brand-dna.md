BRAND DNA DOCUMENT
==================

## BRAND OVERVIEW

**Name:** Cadence
**Tagline:** "Clean Hydration Powered by Salt" / "More than a product, Cadence is a ritual"
**Full Mission Statement:** "Ritual; dedicated to the art and science of hydration, inspired by the rhythm of daily discipline."
**Design Agency:** ADDRESS ARTS (global multidisciplinary design studio; Pentawards 2024 nominee)
**Founded:** 2024, Los Angeles, CA
**Founders:** Ross MacKay (Daring Foods, $325M valuation) + George Heaton (Represent luxury fashion, $100M valuation)

**Voice Adjectives [5]:**
1. Disciplined — every word earns its place; nothing is gratuitous
2. Sophisticated — luxury-tier language with scientific precision
3. Purposeful — every claim is intentional and evidence-backed
4. Minimalist — restraint over noise; negative space as a creative tool
5. Aspirational — speaks to elite athletes and peak-performance professionals

**Positioning:** Premium, salt-powered performance hydration positioned as a daily ritual — not a sports drink. The brand reframes sodium from a demonized compound to the essential mineral powering physical and mental optimization. Cadence occupies the intersection of luxury lifestyle brand and performance supplement.

**Competitive Differentiation:**
- vs. LMNT: RTD aluminum can format vs. powder packets; comparable sodium (500mg) but with lifestyle branding
- vs. Liquid IV: Zero sugar, zero carbs, zero caffeine — "cleaner" formula; more premium design
- vs. Nuun: Far higher sodium content (500mg vs ~300mg); premium price point; luxury brand aesthetic
- vs. Gatorade/traditional: No sugar, science-first formula, anti-mass-market positioning
- Unique advantage: Exclusive distribution at Soho House; celebrity founder credibility; CGI-forward design language

## VISUAL SYSTEM

**Primary Font (Logo Wordmark):** Exposure by 205TF — bold variable typeface, described as "almost illegible weight" with visual grit. The wordmark is the dominant visual element across all packaging. Heavy, commanding, slightly challenging to read — intentional confidence signal.

**Secondary Font (Body / Primary Content):** Editorial New by Pangram Pangram — condensed serif with narrow serifs referencing 1980s–1990s editorial aesthetic. Designed by Mathieu Desjardins (2019). Used for the majority of brand communications: product descriptions, website body, ingredient panels.

**Accent Font (UI / Utilitarian):** Neue Montreal by Pangram Pangram — clean, geometric sans-serif. Designed by Mathieu Desjardins (2018). Used to offset the wordmark weight, providing legibility and balance in digital and print contexts.

**Primary Color:** Black — #000000. Dominant brand color. Used for wordmark, typography, and primary design elements. Signals precision, authority, and premium positioning.

**Secondary Color:** White — #FFFFFF. Background and negative space. Generous white space is a core design principle. Creates the breathing room that makes the brand feel premium vs. cluttered.

**Accent Color (Flavor Indicator):** Green — used in small pops to spell out flavor names on packaging. Specific hex not publicly documented; approximated as a clean, athletic lime-to-mid-green (estimate: #4CAF50 range — a sporty, natural green). Adds energy and visual interest without disrupting the black/white system.

**Melonberry-Specific Color Note:** The Melonberry variant uses a warm berry/watermelon pink-to-coral tone to differentiate from other flavors on the can. Soft, fruity, warm — contrasts cleanly against the black-and-white base.

**Background Colors:** White (#FFFFFF) primary; black (#000000) for high-contrast editorial layouts. No gradients in core brand system.

**CTA Color and Style:** Black button with white text, or white button with black text depending on background. Minimal, flat, no drop shadows. Clean rounded-rectangle or sharp-cornered button — consistent with overall typographic-first aesthetic.

## PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

**Lighting:** Clean studio lighting with soft shadows. For product shots: even, diffused light that reveals the aluminum can's material quality. For lifestyle: natural daylight — morning training sessions, gym environments, outdoor exercise. No harsh flash; premium warmth preferred.

**Color Grading:** Desaturated, near-neutral palette. Black-and-white or low-chroma tones dominant. When color is present (lifestyle contexts), it is muted and natural — no saturated filters. Clean, clinical-adjacent but warm enough to feel aspirational rather than cold.

**Composition:** Typography-first compositions. Product placement is deliberate — often isolated with generous negative space. Close-crop can shots to emphasize material texture and wordmark. Environmental shots position the can as a natural accompaniment to elite-level training, morning rituals, or professional focus contexts.

**Subject Matter:** Elite athletes (CrossFit, running, gym-based), performance professionals, morning routines, post-workout recovery. Product in hand during training or placed on surfaces that signal premium lifestyle: marble counters, gym floors, running tracks, clean wooden desks. Founders and community members used authentically.

**Props and Surfaces:** Marble, raw concrete, brushed metal, wood, clean gym equipment. No cluttered backgrounds. Sparse, intentional prop selection. Ice or condensation on cans to signal refreshment. Black training gear and clean athletic clothing as wardrobe props.

**Mood:** Disciplined restraint. Quiet confidence. Premium without being ostentatious. The visual feel of 5:30am training before the world wakes up — focused, elite, intentional.

## PRODUCT DETAILS: MELONBERRY CORE HYDRATION

**Physical Description:** 355ml (12 fl oz) slim aluminum can. Lightweight, sleek profile — slightly narrower than a standard soda can. Predominantly black background with the bold "CADENCE" Exposure wordmark running large across the label. "CORE HYDRATION" and "MELONBERRY" in Editorial New serif, smaller, positioned below or adjacent to the wordmark. A warm pink/berry-coral color accent indicates the Melonberry flavor variant.

**Label / Logo Placement:** Wordmark dominates the front face of the can — large, bold, centered or slightly high. Flavor name and product descriptor in smaller secondary type below. Minimal additional information on front panel. Nutritional facts and ingredient list on the back/side in clean small type.

**Distinctive Features:**
- Slightly less carbonated than typical sparkling water (intentional — prevents bloating pre-workout)
- Zero calories, zero sugar, zero caffeine — clean nutritional profile visible on label
- 500mg Sodium / 190mg Potassium / 30mg Magnesium callout
- "Core 500" formula designation
- Stevia-sweetened (no artificial sweeteners)
- Vegan, Keto, Paleo certified/compatible

**Packaging System:**
- RTD Cans: 12-pack and 24-pack configurations
- Sachets: 5g powder packets in 30-pack boxes (same formula, portable format)
- Bundle: 24 cans + 30 sachets + branded training bottle
- Flavor range across Core line: Citrus, Cola, Cream Soda, Watermelon Mojito, Melonberry

## AD CREATIVE STYLE

**Typical Formats:** Clean product-hero shots with large typographic headlines. Split-composition layouts with product left and copy right. Testimonial/review cards on solid black or white backgrounds. Lifestyle photography with product in athletic contexts. Very minimal — rarely more than 2-3 design elements on screen.

**Text Overlay Style:** Bold, editorial. Headline in Exposure or a heavy sans-serif approximating that weight. Body copy in Editorial New. Black text on white or white text on black. Very tight letter-spacing on headlines. Short, punchy copy lines — rarely more than 5-7 words. Periods at the end of statements for authority.

**Photo vs. Illustration:** Photography and CGI renders heavily favored. No illustration. Product renders are extremely high-quality CGI with realistic lighting and material fidelity. Lifestyle photography is clean, natural-light editorial.

**UGC Usage:** Emerging — brand is new (2024). Wellworthy reviews and fitness community content visible. When UGC is used, it maintains the premium feel — not raw TikTok content but polished creator-generated material with athletes and fitness professionals.

**Offer Presentation:** Understated. Bundle pricing ($60 for 24+30+bottle) presented factually without aggressive promotional language. Premium brand avoids "SALE" aesthetics. Discounts framed as value or ritual investment, not desperation pricing.
